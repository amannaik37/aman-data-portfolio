The Problem

The business needed to know which customers were likely to churn, and which specific factors were driving that risk — not just a black-box prediction.

The Approach

Cleaned and engineered features across 7,000+ records, handled class imbalance with SMOTE and label encoding, then trained and tuned a Random Forest classifier — evaluating precision and minority-class recall, not just accuracy.

The Result

85% classification accuracy, a ~12% lift in minority-class recall, and a Power BI dashboard with dynamic KPIs, segment filters and drill-through views mapping churn drivers back to retention actions.
